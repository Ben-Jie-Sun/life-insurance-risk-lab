What this build assumes

  • A Yule birth creates a candidate; active customers can die, lapse or expire.
  • Active customers pay at the start of a month. Lapse occurs after covered deaths.
  • Expenses are settled before cash interest; claims are settled at month-end.
  • Unpaid expenses and claims remain liabilities; negative cash is never used to fake a payment.
  • New issues stop after the first unpaid obligation; existing policies are projected to run-off.
  • Reserve floors and the capital buffer are project rules, not statutory measures.
  • Experience shocks affect realized outcomes. Reserves retain the original pricing basis; they are not a stress-revalued solvency measure.
  • Grace periods, real mortality calibration, AI Audit and CAPSAFE are future work.
